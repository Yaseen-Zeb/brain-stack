# Software Engineers in the era of AI
I think we as software engineers should spend more time learning and understanding the basics, programming concepts, what’s happening under the hood, and much more. That’s what really makes us professional developers. There’s a big difference between simply getting a problem solved and actually understanding why and how it works, how to make it more efficient, and how to improve it.

Learning and understanding may take time, but execution doesn’t and shouldn’t have to take as much time anymore. With AI, we can execute things much faster.

And obviously, communication and understanding the real business problem are equally important. Understanding and solving a business problem is one thing, while implementing the solution technically is another. I think having both skills—solving problems from a business perspective and turning those solutions into effective technical implementations—is what truly makes someone a software engineer.
